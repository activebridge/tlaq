First Friday: Kay Nari Paints Live at Creative Gateways Gallery from 4:00–7:00 PM · Creative Gateways Gallery, Suite B125.
Scottsdale abstract painter Kay Nari builds a work from a blank canvas in front of visitors — layer over layer of acrylic and ink — talking through her choices as she goes. Come and go all evening; the finished painting stays at the gallery. Free.
