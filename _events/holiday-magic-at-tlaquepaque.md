Looking to complete your holiday shopping—or simply take a break from the hustle of the season? Join us for Holiday Sweet Stroll at Tlaquepaque Arts & Shopping Village, where the magic of Christmas comes alive.

Browse one-of-a-kind galleries and specialty shops filled with unique gifts for everyone on your holiday list. Whether you're shopping for loved ones or enjoying a festive afternoon with family and friends, Holiday Sweet Stroll offers something for all ages.

Throughout the village, you'll discover beautifully decorated courtyards, sparkling Christmas lights, festive décor, and—new this year—luminaria-lined walkways and courtyards, creating a warm and enchanting holiday atmosphere.

At 5:00 p.m., guests are invited to help light the luminarias throughout Tlaquepaque. Watch as the courtyards and walkways come alive with the warm glow of candlelight, making the village even more magical as evening falls.

Children will delight in visiting Santa Claus, while strolling Dickens-era carolers fill the village with traditional holiday music. Keep an eye out for The Grinch and other beloved holiday characters as they wander through the patios and plazas, ready for fun photos and festive surprises.

Our galleries and shops will remain open late, welcoming guests with:

Sweet treats and homemade Christmas cookies

Decadent chocolate truffles and holiday candies

Hot spiced cider, eggnog, hot chocolate, and seasonal liqueurs

Special holiday discounts and festive giveaways

Families can also enjoy free face painting and Christmas ornament painting for children while they sip on hot chocolate. Local musicians and carolers will provide live entertainment throughout the afternoon, adding to the festive spirit.

Come experience the warmth, beauty, and tradition of the season at Tlaquepaque. We hope Holiday Sweet Stroll becomes a cherished part of your family's holiday celebrations for years to come.
