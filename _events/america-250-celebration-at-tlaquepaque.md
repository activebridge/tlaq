America 250 Celebration at Tlaquepaque

Join Tlaquepaque Arts & Shopping Village and the Oak Creek Canyon Chapter of the Daughters of the American Revolution as we honor Arizona’s celebration of America’s 250th Anniversary.

On Friday, July 3, gather in Patio de las Campanas for a special public reading of the Declaration of Independence, presented in town crier style with period-inspired costumes that bring the spirit of 1776 to life.

This brief but meaningful ceremony invites the community to pause, reflect, and celebrate the ideals of freedom, independence, and the enduring spirit of our nation.

We hope you will join us for this patriotic tribute in the heart of Tlaquepaque.

Date: Friday, July 3
Location: Patio de las Campanas, Tlaquepaque Arts & Shopping Village
Admission: Free and open to the public
