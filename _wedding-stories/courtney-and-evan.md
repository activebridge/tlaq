Courtney Cohen and Evan Krahe were married at Tlaquepaque Arts & Shopping Village on June 13, 2026.

Their story began in Phoenix in 2023, when Evan was there for work. They met online and, with Courtney frequently traveling to the East Coast, began a long-distance relationship that quickly grew into something special. Two years later, they were engaged and began imagining a wedding that would bring their story full circle.

For Courtney, who grew up in Phoenix and had visited Tlaquepaque throughout the years, the village already held special meaning. Its romantic Old World architecture, glowing lanterns, lush landscaping, and charming chapel made it feel like the perfect place to celebrate.

Surrounded by family and friends, Courtney and Evan celebrated a day their guests described as intimate, elegant, and unmistakably them. Tlaquepaque is now part of their story and a place they hope to return to for years to come.
